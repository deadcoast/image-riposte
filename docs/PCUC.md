# MIVIM / Provable Claims and Use Cases

User direction, 2026-09-18: "DEVELOPMENT ROOTED IN `PROVABLE CLAIMS AND USE CASES`
(PCUC)". "Research" supports "Theorizing", then "Formulating", "Critical Self
Critique", "Planning" and "Development", in that nested order. "BREAK THE SUPPORT
CHAIN ANYWHERE NEAR THE TOP AND ITS GOING TO BREAK EVERYTHING ELSE FUNDEMENTALLY
AT A FOUNDATIONAL LEVEL." PAC-034 is explicitly shelved for later development.

This is the development standard for this repository. Its concrete engineering
interpretation below is INFERRED; scientific/product claims require their own proof.

Each development study must retain its supporting primary research, source finding,
falsifiable claim, assumptions, derivation, counterexamples, executable use cases,
measurements and limitations. A failed premise changes the plan. Report actual
results, including losses and unavailable measurements. Separate mathematical
properties, finite-sample observations and external-world effectiveness.

Choose the modules and phases needed by the problem, then finish their integration.
Do not pad source with demonstrations or call novelty/leadership established by
aspiration. A proof about a proxy gradient is not a proof of protection efficacy.
Quantization surrogates, quality gates, model identity and real misuse-task outcomes
must remain explicit. Optimize the real production path while keeping consent,
private artwork handling and exact-file review intact.

The first study is [shared publication views](../evidence/pcuc-shared-views/README.md):
factor a repeated vector-Jacobian calculation across model contexts, retain one
model graph at a time, and measure equivalent gradients and reduced transform work.
Its phases and future questions live in `todo-audit.PACER.md` during the CSV freeze.

The second study is [committed generation experiments](../evidence/pcuc-experiments/README.md):
seal hypotheses' operational endpoints and analysis before generation, preserve
failed attempts, conceal arm labels in review packets and record exact-artifact
task decisions. The arithmetic is tested exhaustively for binary arm outcomes;
local commitments and descriptive results do not establish independent
preregistration, model qualification or population efficacy.

The third study is [committed artwork cohorts](../evidence/pcuc-cohorts/README.md):
fix the sampling frame and selected artwork denominator, retain failures and
primary-review order, and derive exact bounds for missing outcomes. Unit-level
aggregation prevents seed/control counts from masquerading as more artworks;
finite-sample bounds expose missing evidence without an independence assumption.

The fourth study is [reviewer calibration panels](../evidence/pcuc-review-panels/README.md):
freeze the selected outputs, task rubric and reviewer slots before packet export;
retain each final response separately; derive tight completion bounds for binary
agreement and show pairwise chance-corrected diagnostics with their underlying
counts. Agreement measures consistency on these outputs, not correctness,
population reliability or protection efficacy.

The fifth study is [local Kontext editing](../evidence/pcuc-kontext/README.md):
add one fixed modern editing architecture with named-asset model identity and
explicit task conditioning. Exact aspect-ratio selection and packed-latent geometry
bind measured prepared pixels to pipeline inputs and output dimensions. A tiny
randomly initialized real library pipeline checks context-token flow, repeatability
and input sensitivity on CPU. Pretrained editing and CUDA qualification remain
separate, unavailable gates.
